MORE THAN A MEAL
Every Dish Has a Story

Open index.html to visit the exhibition. For imported models or to preview
the site before hosting it, open a terminal in this folder and run
python -m http.server 8000
Then visit http://localhost:8000. Deploy the entire folder to a static host.

The original A and B generator pages are included, along with their existing
preview, export, and game scripts. The exhibition uses the same Three.js r147
renderer, procedural sea island geometry, orbit camera, and raycaster.
The renderer accepts an optional scenery hook for the exhibition.

Edit exhibition/content.js for station text, research findings, country names,
dishes, quotations and media. Paths are relative to index.html. Leave optional
video or model fields as empty strings until assets are ready. Use H.264 MP4
for broad playback support and supply captions with the final video assets.
For GLB or glTF models, keep associated textures and buffers in their original
relative locations. The bundled loader supports standard, uncompressed
GLB/glTF. Models are centered and fitted to the display table. A failed load
keeps the stylized 3D placeholder.

The seven dishes have stylized 3D displays and credited photographs. In each
dish entry in exhibition/content.js, edit intro and background for exhibit
copy, quote and quoteContext for personal survey responses, photos for
image paths, credits and licenses, video for the optional MP4, and model
for a GLB or glTF replacement. Country entries contain names and flag paths.

Add the research infographic or verified findings, the welcome MP4,
the two interview recordings, optional dish videos, and any custom food
models. The exhibition uses the supplied survey quotations. Flags are
from Flagcdn and photo credits and source links appear in the galleries.

The country pavilions are stylized references to everyday architecture
and markets. Decorative bands use original geometric patterns.

Three timber footbridges connect the main island to the country islands.
The built-in walkers follow their island loops and stay off the bridges.
The exhibition loads the six shipped flag designs into WebGL from
exhibition/flag-pixels.js. For a replacement flag, add a new image
filename and set its path in exhibition/content.js so the original
bundled flag texture is not selected.

For the optional analytics and Google Forms feedback integrations, see
ANALYTICS AND FEEDBACK SETUP.md. Add the real Measurement ID and responder
link in exhibition/content.js before publishing to GitHub Pages.

See HOW TO ADD MY CONTENT.md for the asset folders, editable media paths,
supported file formats, and local preview instructions.

VISITOR ACTIVITIES

Open Food Passport from the floating button. Opening a dish awards its
country's stamp once. Either Taiwan dish qualifies. The browser remembers
the six stamp slots, and Reset passport asks before clearing them.

Use the main island's quiz sign or guide to answer five questions and
see the score. Retry quiz starts a fresh round. Edit the questions,
explanations and passport illustrations in exhibition/content.js.

Use the Reflection Wall sign or guide to write up to 300 characters.
Save reflection updates the private note in this browser. Open the
wall again to edit or delete it. Notes are never published or sent to
analytics. Browser storage may be unavailable in some private modes.
